World Concept

After a long an advanced evolution process, animals that once inhabit oceans, rivers and lakes have come to live ashore in humanoid form. Set in a medieval fantasy world where the tides of the past still shape society, culture, and conflict.

Narrative Tone

Serious Core

Government conflicts. High stakes at hand. Ruling a kingdom is no easy task, and characters struggle to anchor their perception of the world in a solid view.

Romantic Threads

Relationships bloom amidst tensions. Can two completely opposite species find a workaround? Is true love a risk, or fate?

Humor Moments

Levity surfaces naturally through character quirks and cultural clashes. BURN THE PIRATES! SAY NO TO PLUNDERING!
